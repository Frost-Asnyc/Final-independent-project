Our Mission

To make premium car rental accessible, transparent, and hassle-free for every Kenyan — whether you're heading to a boardroom or a bush adventure.

Our Vision

To be East Africa's most trusted automotive rental brand, known for exceptional service, world-class vehicles, and a team that genuinely cares.

Our Story

ASNYC Rentals Ltd was founded in Nairobi by Lerionka Olentiki with a simple but powerful mission — make premium car rental accessible to everyone. What started as a small fleet of 5 vehicles has grown into one of Nairobi's most trusted and recognised rental services.

Lerionka believed that how you get there matters just as much as the destination. Every vehicle in our fleet is hand-picked, regularly serviced, and fully insured so you can focus entirely on the journey ahead.

Whether you need a car for a day, a week, or a month — we've got you covered with transparent pricing and absolutely zero hidden fees.
